# x126
I am triyn to make this amazing rc plane , i had this dream when i was small , one day to make my own plane and drop waterballons and have fun , this dream is slowly coming real but i need funding , and this will serve as future generations of (as in design platforms) planes(rc),guided rockets(rc) and rocket powered planes as of now its in a early development stage just a wing :)

This project gave me an understanding of how airplanes are designed. It also taught me that engineering involves more than building something: it requires research, testing ideas solving problems and improving the design step by step.

I hope to continue improving the airplane design and learn more, about aerodynamics, propulsion and aircraft stability.

materials
1400kv motor 
9g servos (4)
NX4 gyroscope 
flysky 6ch receiver
any 6ch transmitter
lipo battery 2200 mah 

Dimensions 
wing width 15cm 
wingspan 75cm
fuselage after wings 30cm
alerion length 19cm 
        width 3cm
propeller 10 inch 
total fuselage 40cm
